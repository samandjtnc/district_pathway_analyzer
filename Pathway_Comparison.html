⚠️ Critical Problems:
  • ❌ Premature specialization - Students locked into narrow tracks before understanding the field
  • ❌ No shared foundation - Missing cross-cutting concepts (AI, data, systems thinking)
  • ❌ Parallel silos - Four separate entry points create confusion and fragmentation
  • ❌ Limited switching - Hard to change tracks once started (Python → Design requires starting over)
  • ❌ Missing modern topics - No exposure to AI, machine learning, data science, or cybersecurity
  • ❌ Student confusion - "How do I know which track is right if I haven't tried any?"

✅ Transformative Benefits:

  • ✅ Shared foundation - All students get AI, data, systems, networks, cybersecurity basics

The Transformation: Same Courses, Radically Different Student Experience
❌ Before: Parallel Silos
Student at Grade 9: "I have to pick between Python, Design, Networks, or Web. I don't know what any of these really are. My friend likes coding, so I guess I'll do Python too..."

Student at Grade 11: "I hate just writing code. I wish I'd known about cybersecurity or AI. But I'm two years into Python—too late to switch now."

Result: Accidental pathways, narrow skills, student frustration, high course-switching.
✅ After: Exploration → Choice
Student at Grade 9: "In AI Foundations, I'm learning Python, networks, data, AI, cybersecurity. I love the network security stuff—that's my thing!"

Student at Grade 11: "I chose Network Engineering because I discovered I love it in grade 9. And I can still use my Python skills to automate networks. I know where I'm headed."

Result: Confident choices, well-rounded skills, student engagement, clear career paths.
